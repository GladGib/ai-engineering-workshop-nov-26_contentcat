# Platform House Rules (our internal defaults, not official platform specs)

| Platform | Aspect | Target length | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 1:1 or 4:5 | 30–90 s | ≤ 3 short lines + 3–5 hashtags |
| Reels | 9:16 | 15–30 s | ≤ 150 chars |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 1–3 min explainer | ≤ 300 chars |

- Always burn in subtitles (most LinkedIn video is watched muted).
- Every script lists beats with timestamps that add up to the target length.
- On-screen text must be readable in under 1.5 s.
