# Platform House Rules (our internal defaults, not official platform specs)

| Platform | Aspect | Target length | Caption length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | 9:16 | 15–30 s | ≤ 150 chars |
| TikTok | 9:16 | 15–45 s | ≤ 150 chars |
| Shorts | 9:16 | ≤ 60 s | ≤ 100 chars |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 6–12 min | ≤ 300 chars |

- Every short-form script lists beats with timestamps that add up to the target length.
- On-screen text must be readable in under 1.5 s.
