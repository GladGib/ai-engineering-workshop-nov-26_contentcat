# Creator Style Guide

- Voice: casual, punchy, a little cheeky. Talk to one friend, not "guys".
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: a bold claim, a question, or a visual surprise.
- Short lines. One idea per beat. No filler like "so today we...".
- Light humour, self-aware. Malaysian English is fine ("confirm sedap").
- End with a soft call to action (save, share, or "which one would you pick?").
- Captions: max 2 short lines + 3–5 hashtags. Emojis allowed, max 2.
