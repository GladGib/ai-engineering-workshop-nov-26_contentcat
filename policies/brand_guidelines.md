# DELMIA Quintiq Social Brand Guidelines (demo, internal)

- Audience: supply chain leaders, planners, operations and IT managers.
- Voice: confident, clear, practical. Lead with a real planning pain (disruptions, manual spreadsheets, last-minute re-plans), then show the outcome.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: a relatable problem or a sharp question ("What happens to your plan when a machine goes down at 9am?").
- Talk about capabilities: production planning & scheduling, logistics planning, workforce planning, what-if scenarios, KPI-driven optimization, virtual twin.
- **Compliance:** no performance numbers or percentages unless the user supplies an approved source. Never name or show a customer without written approval.
- No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing"). No competitor mentions.
- Captions: 1–3 short lines, end with a soft CTA (e.g. "Watch the full demo" / "How does your team re-plan?").
- Hashtags (3–5): #DELMIA #Quintiq #SupplyChain #SupplyChainPlanning #Optimization #Manufacturing #Logistics
